What Claude is asked, per page#

Six calls, all on the shared pause and daily budget (Claude ingest).

        flowchart LR
    classDef claude fill:#fff3e0,stroke:#e08a00
    classDef big fill:#fde8e8,stroke:#c0504d

    PH["Photo reading"]:::big --> WU["Write-up"]:::big
    EN["Enrich"]:::claude --> RE["Related"]:::claude
    WU --> RE
    TD["Topic discovery"]:::big --> TS["Topic synthesis"]:::big
    TS --> RE

Arrows show what queues what; red calls use the vision model, orange the text model. Photo reading, the write-up and the two topic calls use SCRAP_VISION_MODEL (default claude-opus-5-5); enrich and related use SCRAP_TEXT_MODEL (default claude-haiku-5-5). Ask Claude (POST /api/pages/{id}/enrich) runs enrich, then on a board page the write-up (on a topic page its synthesis), then related, on any page. Topic discovery is one call over the whole library, not a page’s: see the end. “Nightly” is the related-pages job and “weekly” the topics job, both in Scheduled jobs.

What each call sees. Enrich gets the page’s kind, tags, title and text as plain words (at most 12,000 characters), what was read from its images (at most 8,000) and, for a link, the fetched text. Related gets the page in brief (title, tags, summary, the first 2,000 characters of its text and 3,000 of its images’ text) and an index of up to 150 candidates. The candidates are the pages sharing the most tags, then a full-text search for 24 of the page’s words: title, tags and summary first, then its images’ text, their descriptions, and last its own text. Data is quoted in <page>, <images> and <fetched> blocks, and any such tag inside the data has its < sent as &lt;, so the data cannot end its block. A photo page usually has no enrich summary (Ask can add one); a board’s write-up gives it one. The write-up gets the board photos themselves (at most 4), what was read from them (at most 12,000 characters), the page’s title, tags and text (at most 6,000), the owner’s guidance in a <guidance> block and the related call’s index; it cites pages by number, and scrap makes those [[slug]] links (Write-ups of boards).

The store’s health checks (is:orphan, is:outdated and the rest, the PWA’s Store health screen) are plain SQL and make no call: Claude’s views of the library are the topic syntheses (contradictions and open questions), topic discovery (topics to make) and the related pass (links to suggest) (29. Store health checks as search filters; outdated write-ups are the owner’s to regenerate).

Imports are not read as text unless scrap enrich --backfill queues them (their first enrich then queues the related call). Their photos are read only with scrap import --ingest.

Topic discovery (scrap enrich --topics, weekly: Scheduled jobs) is one call over the whole library: an index of every page not in the trash and not a topic (at most 2,000, the most recently changed: number, title, up to 6 tags, a summary cut to 150 characters), the existing topics with their sources’ numbers, and the keys of the topics in the trash (rejected, never made again). It returns new topics (at most 5 a pass, each with at least 2 sources) and new sources for existing topics, by number only; new topics are made at once, then each topic due is queued for its synthesis. A topic’s synthesis gets each source’s title, kind and tags, its summary, Claude’s write-up of it, what was read from its images and its text (at most 6,000 characters a source and 60,000 in all), in numbered <source> blocks, the owner’s notes in a <notes> block and guidance in a <guidance> block; it cites sources by number, made [[slug]] links as a write-up’s are (Topic pages). Neither writes into any page’s text.

Page roles#

What a page is decides what automation may do to it. Every test of it is in one module, store/roles.py, and this table matches it row for row. The page’s first capture decides; later captures (photos added to it) only add content (Added photos: the first capture decides). The owner can always change the kind, title and tags by hand.

Open questions, marked TODO in store/roles.py for the owner (none changes what scrap does today):

  • Board or topic first: a page that is both is a board in writeup_type and the write-up view, but a topic for is:outdated, a related page’s via and Regenerate. No page can be both: a topic takes no photos and has no captures.
