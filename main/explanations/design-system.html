The PWA’s design system#

The phone app is a tool you reach for one-handed, often in a hurry: open, capture, gone. The design follows from that, and from keeping the app tiny (no UI framework, no icon library, no web font). Everything visual is defined once, in web/src/tokens.css; the components in web/src/style.css use nothing else. This page is the portable description, for carrying the design into another tool and back.

Principles#

  • Paper and ink, one colour. Warm off-white paper and warm black ink in daylight, a warm near-black and cream at night, and a single amber (a sticky note: scrap’s mark). Amber means “the thing to press” or “this one is selected”; red means only “destructive” or “failed”. No blue-greys, no second accent.

  • One hero per screen. Composer and editor: Save, the one amber fill. Capture: no hero; its three actions (Camera, Gallery, Note) are equal tiles, since none is more likely than the others. Page: its toolbar. Everything else steps down: secondary (outlined surface), then ghost (text only).

  • Under the thumb. Capture actions, the page toolbar and the nav sit at the bottom; Cancel, Preview and Save sit in the editor’s top bar because the keyboard owns the bottom.

  • Icons with words. Icons help find a control; the word says what it does. Icon-only buttons (close, previous/next photo, remove a photo) carry an aria-label.

  • Calm states. Empty, offline and error states are an icon, one plain sentence and at most one action. On the capture screen a problem is a quiet inline line, never a whole screen.

  • Dark is designed, not inverted. Surfaces get lighter as they come forward; amber is a step lighter; toasts stay dark slabs in both themes.

  • Every gesture has a button. Swipes are shortcuts, never the only way: a row’s Edit and Delete are also on its page (and a row’s tick is a button of its own, with Done and Reopen on the page too), the page’s swipe is also its ‹ › pager and the arrow keys. Gestures start away from the screen’s edges, which belong to the phone’s Back gesture.

  • Motion confirms, never decorates. 90-260 ms, eased out; none under reduced motion.

Tokens#

Light / dark values, held to WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for control edges) twice: web/tests/contrast.test.ts reads tokens.css and checks the listed text/background pairs, every style.css rule that sets both a text colour and a background from tokens, and white text on --overlay over a white photo; and an end-to-end sweep (web/e2e/view.spec.ts) computes the real colour of every visible piece of text and of what lies behind it, on each screen in both themes.

Presses, edges and washes that need a translucent tint mix a token with transparent (color-mix(in srgb, var(--ink) 14%, transparent)) rather than writing a new colour.

Type: the system UI face (Roboto on Android); 17 px root. --t-xs 0.75rem (labels under icons, chips), --t-sm 0.875rem (meta, small buttons), --t-md 1rem (body, list titles), --t-lg 1.1875rem (wordmark, field titles, Markdown headings), --t-xl 1.5rem (page title). Line height 1.5, 1.25 for titles, 1.6 for page text. Weights 500 (controls), 600 (titles, labels), 700 (page title, active nav).

Space: a 4 px grid, --s-1..--s-7 = 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48. Screen gutter 16. Radius: --r-sm 8 (thumbnails, code), --r-md 12 (buttons, fields, toasts), --r-lg 16 (cards, lists, photos), --r-pill (badge, search field, nav indicator). Size: --tap 48 (every touch target, extended with an invisible ::after where the drawn control is smaller), --bar-h 56, --nav-h 64. Elevation: --shadow (selected segment), --shadow-2 (capture cluster, toasts). Motion: --d-1 90 ms (press), --d-2 180 ms (view enter, toast), --d-3 260 ms (editor sheet, viewer); --ease cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). Under prefers-reduced-motion all three are 0 and the placeholder shimmer stops.

States#

State

How it shows

Pressed

scale 0.97 over --d-1 and a --sunk fill (primary: 6 % darker; tiles: --accent-soft); the browser’s tap flash is off

Focus (keyboard)

2 px --accent-ink outline, 2 px offset; fields draw it inside their edge

Selected

segmented: raised --surface with --shadow and an amber icon; nav: --accent-soft pill, ink label, bold

Disabled

50 % opacity (exempt from contrast, and never the only sign: the label says why)

Busy

the label reads “Preparing…” and taps are ignored; no dimming, so it stays at full contrast

Loading image

the reserved box shimmers between --sunk and --line until the image loads or fails, then stops

Failed

--danger text in words, a --danger-soft badge; never colour alone

Layout and breakpoints#

  • Portrait phones (300 px and up): the default. Under 380 px wide the segmented control drops its icons.

  • Short screens (under 500 px high, i.e. landscape or a large text size): top bar and nav 48 px, and the page toolbar scrolls with the page instead of sticking. Landscape (also 560 px and wider): the nav’s label sits beside its icon and the capture tiles are 64 high.

  • Very narrow (under 300 px: a small phone at its largest text size): the capture cluster scrolls with the list instead of covering it, tiles go two by two, labels and list titles wrap, list thumbnails shrink to 32, and toolbars show icons only (the words remain their accessible names).

  • Toasts sit just above whatever is under the thumb (the capture cluster, the page toolbar, the viewer’s tools or the nav), clear of the newest row and the title; in the editor, just under its top bar.

  • Text that may be right-to-left (titles, page text, snippets, the editor) has dir="auto", and spacing uses logical properties (margin-inline-*, padding-inline-start).

  • A page photo reserves a 4:3 box while it loads, then takes the photo’s own shape (aspect-ratio: auto 4 / 3), at most 75 % of the screen high.

High contrast#

Under forced-colors: active the system replaces fills and drops shadows, so the selected segment and the current nav item get a 2 px Highlight outline, and the borderless tiles and badge get a ButtonText edge.

Icons#

Sizes: 20 in buttons, 24 in tiles, the nav and list placeholders, 18 in the segmented control, 16 on a photo’s remove button, 52 (in a circle) in empty states. Icons: camera, image, note, todo (check in a square; Done, and a row’s tick, which hides the check until done), later (bookmark), board (a whiteboard on its stand), link, file, download, share, edit, trash, search, close, back, next, fit, retry (also Reopen), offline; the nav’s plus and clock are inline in index.html. The app icon (web/public/icon.svg, PNGs by npm run icons) is the paper scrap on amber; the header mark is the same scrap in amber.

What we don’t do#

  • No web font, icon font, CSS framework or component library.

  • No colour outside tokens.css (the debug panel aside), and no second accent; no gradients except the viewer’s scrim. High-contrast mode uses the system’s colour keywords.

  • No colour as the only signal: selection also raises, failure also says so in words.

  • No modal dialogs of our own (the browser’s confirm() stays for destructive questions); the share menu is a light-dismiss popover, not a modal.

  • No motion longer than 260 ms, no motion that carries meaning, none under reduced motion.

  • No touch target under 48 px (the 36 px badge, the 30 px remove button and the 42 px segments extend their hit area with an invisible ::after), no text under 12.75 px, no text pair under AA.

  • No dimming to mean “busy”: opacity would break contrast; the label says it instead.
