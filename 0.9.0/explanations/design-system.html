The PWA’s design system#

The phone app is a tool you reach for one-handed, often in a hurry: open, capture, gone. The design follows from that, and from keeping the app tiny: the shell (capture, lists, pages, search) uses no UI framework, icon library or web font, and its JavaScript is held under 16 kB gzipped (npm run size, web/scripts/size.mjs). The one exception is the drawing viewer for a photo’s drawing: a separate chunk, fetched only when a drawing is shown, built on React and Excalidraw with Excalidraw’s own fonts (served by scrap from /excalidraw/fonts/), and outside that budget. Everything visual is defined once, in web/src/tokens.css; the components in web/src/style.css (which imports web/src/css/*.css) and web/src/excalidraw.css (the photo panel and the drawing viewer) use nothing else. This page is the portable description, for carrying the design into another tool and back.

Principles#

  • Paper and ink, one colour. Warm off-white paper and warm black ink in daylight, a warm near-black and cream at night, and a single amber (a sticky note: scrap’s mark). Amber means “the thing to press” or “this one is selected”; red means only “destructive” or “failed”. No blue-greys, no second accent.

  • One hero per screen. Composer and editor: Save, the one amber fill. Todos: no hero (New todo is a quiet + in the top bar). Capture: no hero; its three actions (Camera, Gallery, Note) are equal tiles, since none is more likely than the others. Page: a todo’s or a Later’s Mark done, the toolbar’s one fill; a page with no status has none. Everything else steps down: secondary (outlined surface), then ghost (text only).

  • Under the thumb. Capture actions and the nav sit at the bottom; a page has no nav, so its toolbar is the only bottom bar. On a computer (a mouse and a window 900 px wide or more: css/desktop.css, one media query, so a phone or a touch tablet never changes) things go where the eye and the mouse already are instead: the nav is tabs in the middle of the top bar (icon beside its word, the current one on --accent-soft, the Todos count after the word), Capture’s tiles are a row over the list, a page’s actions are a row of buttons under its title, tags, due line and meta line, kept in view under the top bar as it scrolls (Mark done filled, Delete apart at the end), toasts sit at the window’s foot as wide as they need, and rows, chips, tiles and ghost buttons take --sunk on hover. Cancel, Preview and Save sit in the editor’s top bar because the keyboard owns the bottom.

  • Drill down, then Back, named. A page is reached from somewhere. On every phone and browser the top bar swaps the wordmark for Back named for where it goes (‹ Search, ‹ Todos, ‹ Capture), as a mail app’s ‹ Inbox: it goes straight to the list the page was opened from, past pages stepped through or followed, while the phone’s own Back goes one step at a time; opened cold from a link, it goes up to a parent (Todos for a todo, Capture for any other page).

  • Icons with words. Icons help find a control; the word says what it does. Icon-only buttons (close, previous/next photo, remove a photo) carry an aria-label.

  • Calm states. Empty, offline and error states are an icon, one plain sentence and at most one action. On the capture screen a problem is a quiet inline line, never a whole screen; offline there is the badge, the waiting rows and one line, with no card, Retry or toast.

  • Say it once. A row shows its kind by its icon alone; a chip only for a status that ends it (done, cancelled). A page says its kind and status once, in its meta line’s chip.

  • Dark is designed, not inverted. Surfaces get lighter as they come forward; amber is a step lighter; toasts stay dark slabs in both themes.

  • Every gesture has a button. Swipes are shortcuts, never the only way: a row’s Edit and Delete are also on its page (and a row’s tick is a button of its own, with Mark done and Reopen on the page too), the page’s swipe is also its ∧ ∨ pager and the arrow keys. Gestures start away from the screen’s edges, which belong to the phone’s Back gesture.

  • Motion confirms, never decorates. 90-260 ms, eased out; none under reduced motion. The two looping indicators (busy, loading image) are the only motion that runs longer.

Tokens#

Light / dark values, held to WCAG AA (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for control edges) twice: web/tests/contrast.test.ts reads tokens.css and checks the listed text/background pairs, every rule in web/src/css/ that sets both a text colour and a background from tokens, and white text on --overlay over a white photo; and an end-to-end sweep (the lowContrast helper in web/e2e/contrast.ts, run by web/e2e/view.spec.ts on each screen in both themes and by several other e2e specs) computes the real colour of every visible piece of text and of what lies behind it.

The theme follows the system unless Settings (the top bar’s cog) says otherwise (24. A settings pane in the top bar, with theme overrides): Light or Dark sets data-theme on <html>, which tokens.css reads in place of the media query (web/src/theme.ts). Drawings have a theme of their own, Light by default.

The browser’s theme-color follows the colour scheme: two <meta name="theme-color"> tags in web/index.html, one per scheme, with the --bg values below (theme.ts sets both to the chosen one when the theme is forced). The PWA manifest (web/public/manifest.webmanifest) does not: its theme_color and background_color are a fixed dark #161411.

While it renders it says “Loading drawing…”; if it can’t, “Can’t show the drawing here: tap to open it”, or offline “Offline: the drawing needs the server”</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-even"><td><p>Drawing viewer</p></td> <td><p><code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.drawing-layer</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.drawing-bar</span></code></p></td> <td><p>the full-screen drawing (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">board/viewer.ts</span></code>), a <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">role="dialog"</span></code> <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aria-modal</span></code> sheet over the app (the nav is hidden) that rises in over <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--d-3</span></code>, on <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--bg</span></code>. Under the bar, a live-region <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.drawing-notes</span></code> (at most 40 % of the height) holds the restored-draft and conflict banners (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.restored</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.conflict</span></code>) and History (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.versions</span></code>: a 48 px row per version, <strong>Restore</strong>). The canvas and its tools are Excalidraw’s own</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-odd"><td><p>Photo viewer</p></td> <td><p><code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.viewer</span></code></p></td> <td><p><code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--viewer</span></code>; close top right, count pill top centre, tools on a scrim that fades from clear to <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--overlay</span></code></p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-even"><td><p>Icons</p></td> <td><p><code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">icons.ts</span></code></p></td> <td><p>24 px strokes (1.8), currentColor, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aria-hidden</span></code></p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-odd"><td><p>Markdown</p></td> <td><p><code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.md</span></code> (page text, Preview)</p></td> <td><p>headings a step down a level (see Type), all under the page title; on a Later page, a link alone on a line is a link card (above); code and a table’s head on <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--sunk</span></code>; a table in a <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.table</span></code> box with <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--line</span></code> hairlines that scrolls sideways on its own (the page never does); task boxes in <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--accent</span></code>, in the bullet’s place, the list as tight as a bulleted one; each box in a <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.taskbox</span></code> label with a target <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--tap</span></code> (48 px) wide and one line high, so no two overlap; each box is named by its item’s text (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aria-label</span></code>); on a page they tick in place (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">tasks.ts</span></code>: each box’s <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">data-line</span></code> is its item’s line in the text, from markdown-it’s map), off while offline and in Preview; strikethrough, quotes and footnotes in <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--ink-2</span></code>; a Mermaid <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.diagram</span></code> in its dark or neutral theme, following the system’s</p></td> </tr> </tbody> </table> </div> </section> <section id="states"> <h2>States<a class="headerlink" href="#states" title="Link to this heading">#</a></h2> <div class="pst-scrollable-table-container"><table class="table"> <thead> <tr class="row-odd"><th class="head"><p>State</p></th> <th class="head"><p>How it shows</p></th> </tr> </thead> <tbody> <tr class="row-even"><td><p>Pressed</p></td> <td><p>scale 0.97 over <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--d-1</span></code> and a <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--sunk</span></code> fill (primary: 6 % darker; tiles: <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--accent-soft</span></code>); the browser’s tap flash is off</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-odd"><td><p>Focus (keyboard)</p></td> <td><p>2 px <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--accent-ink</span></code> outline, 2 px offset; fields draw it inside their edge</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-even"><td><p>Selected</p></td> <td><p>segmented: raised <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--surface</span></code> with <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--shadow</span></code> and an amber icon; nav: <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--accent-soft</span></code> pill, ink label, bold</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-odd"><td><p>Disabled</p></td> <td><p>50 % opacity (exempt from contrast, and never the only sign: the label says why)</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-even"><td><p>Busy</p></td> <td><p>the label reads “Preparing…” and taps are ignored; the same page again does not blank)</p></td> </tr> <tr class="row-odd"><td><p>Not found</p></td> <td><p>an empty state with the search icon, “Not found: it may have been deleted.” and <strong>Back to capture</strong>; back to <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">--ink-2</span></code> once done</p></td> </tr> </tbody> </table> </div> </section> <section id="the-filters-panel-in-place-not-a-modal"> <h2>The Filters panel: in place, not a modal<a class="headerlink" href="#the-filters-panel-in-place-not-a-modal" title="Link to this heading">#</a></h2> <p>The refine row kept gaining controls at its left end (Clear, Tags, Sort, the count, then every filter chosen), pushing what you came for off the screen. Those controls are used now and then, not on every search, so they moved behind one <strong>Filters</strong> button. For a while it opened a bottom sheet (a modal <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre"><dialog></span></code>), but a sheet hides the list its choices change, needs its own Back handling (a history entry of its own, stepped over on Forward) and costs a tap to close after every few taps. So the filters open in place instead:</p> <ul class="simple"> <li><p><strong>Filters</strong> is a slider icon at the end of the search field, where the thumb already is; the number of filters on rides on it. A tap opens or folds the panel (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aria-expanded</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aria-controls</span></code>); it stays open or folded while the app is open, the next search too.</p></li> <li><p>The panel opens under the search field and the filters on, above the kind row and the list, so each tap in it shows its effect at once. A row per kind of choice (Tags, Order, When), each one line that scrolls sideways, keeps it short: never a wall of chips. The tags it offers are capped at 8 when typing (12 for a search’s top tags). Typing matches the tags of the results (of every tag in use before a search), so there is no separate screen to browse them (the Tags screen is retired; <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">/tags</span></code> opens Search).</p></li> <li><p>What is on is always in view, panel open or not: a row of chips under the field, each with a ✕. The kind row stays under it, always shown.</p></li> <li><p>A tap changes the query at once, so the panel is never a form to submit and never holds state the address doesn’t: Back, a reload or a shared link show the same list. A control keeps its focus as the panel is drawn again.</p></li> <li><p>It is <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">refine.ts</span></code>, a chunk of its own loaded with the search screen and kept offline by the service worker, so the app shell stays small.</p></li> </ul> </section> <section id="layout-and-breakpoints"> <h2>Layout and breakpoints<a class="headerlink" href="#layout-and-breakpoints" title="Link to this heading">#</a></h2> <ul class="simple"> <li><p>Portrait phones (300 px and up): the default. Under 380 px wide a segmented control drops its icons, except the kind rows (search’s and the Todos screen’s) and the editor’s kind picker, which keep theirs over the word.</p></li> <li><p>A wide window (760 px and up: a laptop, a tablet on its side): one centred column of 720 px (the top bar, the screen, the nav and the editor), the background still to the edges.</p></li> <li><p>Larger text (iPhone’s Larger Text at 150 % or 200 %): the kind rows and the kind picker wrap instead of clipping, the Filters tag box fits, and the nav grows with its labels. Left for later at 200 %: the page toolbar wraps Delete to a second row, the editor’s top bar is tight, its folds push the kind picker off-screen, and Capture’s tiles leave about two rows of the list in view.</p></li> <li><p>Short screens (under 500 px high, i.e. landscape or a large text size): top bar and nav 48 px, and the page toolbar scrolls with the page instead of sticking. Landscape (also 560 px and wider): the nav’s label sits beside its icon and the capture tiles are 64 high.</p></li> <li><p>Very narrow (under 300 px: a small phone at its largest text size): the capture cluster scrolls with the list instead of covering it (it is static and moves to the top, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">order:</span> <span class="pre">-1</span></code>, so Camera is on the first screen and the list follows), tiles go two by two, labels and list titles wrap, list thumbnails shrink to 32, and toolbars show icons only (the words remain their accessible names).</p></li> <li><p>Toasts sit just above whatever is under the thumb (the capture cluster, the page toolbar, the viewer’s tools or the nav), clear of the newest row and the title; in the editor, just under its top bar.</p></li> <li><p>Text that may be right-to-left (titles, page text, snippets, the editor) has <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">dir="auto"</span></code>, and spacing uses logical properties (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">margin-inline-*</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">padding-inline-start</span></code>).</p></li> <li><p>A page photo reserves a 4:3 box while it loads, then takes the photo’s own shape (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aspect-ratio:</span> <span class="pre">auto</span> <span class="pre">4</span> <span class="pre">/</span> <span class="pre">3</span></code>), at most 75 % of the screen high.</p></li> </ul> </section> <section id="high-contrast"> <h2>High contrast<a class="headerlink" href="#high-contrast" title="Link to this heading">#</a></h2> <p>Under <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">forced-colors:</span> <span class="pre">active</span></code> the system replaces fills and drops shadows, so the selected segment and the current nav item get a 2 px <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">Highlight</span></code> outline, and the borderless tiles and badge get a <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">ButtonText</span></code> edge. In the drawing viewer, the chosen Photo / Drawing tab and a pressed toggle in its bar get a 3 px <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">Highlight</span></code> outline.</p> </section> <section id="icons"> <h2>Icons<a class="headerlink" href="#icons" title="Link to this heading">#</a></h2> <p>Sizes: 20 in buttons, 24 in tiles, the nav and list placeholders, 18 in the segmented control, 16 on a photo’s remove button, 52 (in a circle) in empty states. Icons: camera, image, note, tag (the Tags pill and the Tags fold), todo (a checklist: the Todo kind, so a row’s kind icon never reads as a ticked box beside its tick box), tick (check in a square: a row’s tick, which hides the check until done, and the Todos empty state; the nav’s Todos tab draws the same box inline), done (a check alone: Mark done, so a box always means a tick you can see), add (a plus: New todo), due (a calendar: a todo’s due pill, the Due fold, Upcoming), now (a clock: Now), all (four tiles: All, on the kind row and the Todos screen), later (bookmark: Later), board (a whiteboard on its stand), link, open (an arrow out of a box: a link card’s Open), file, download, share, edit, trash, search, filter (two sliders: the Filters chip), close, back, next, fit, retry (also Reopen), offline; the editor’s keys draw their own (an empty box, a bulleted and a numbered list, lines moved in and out); the nav’s plus, check box and magnifier are inline in <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">index.html</span></code>. The app icon (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">web/public/icon.svg</span></code>, PNGs by <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">npm</span> <span class="pre">run</span> <span class="pre">icons</span></code>) is the paper scrap on amber; the header mark is the same scrap in amber.</p> </section> <section id="what-we-don-t-do"> <h2>What we don’t do<a class="headerlink" href="#what-we-don-t-do" title="Link to this heading">#</a></h2> <ul class="simple"> <li><p>No web font, icon font, CSS framework or component library in the shell. The drawing viewer is a separate lazily loaded chunk on React and Excalidraw, with Excalidraw’s bundled fonts, and is outside the 16 kB shell budget.</p></li> <li><p>No colour outside <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">tokens.css</span></code> (the debug panel aside), and no second accent; no gradients except the photo viewer’s scrim and the fade mask at the end of a sideways-scrolling row (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.tags</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.refine</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.toptags</span></code>: a 32 px <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">mask-image</span></code> fade to transparent, not a colour). High-contrast mode uses the system’s colour keywords.</p></li> <li><p>No colour as the only signal: selection also raises, failure also says so in words.</p></li> <li><p>No sheet or dialog for choices: the Filters panel opens in place (above), the browser’s <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">confirm()</span></code> stays for destructive questions, and the share menu is a light-dismiss popover. The full-screen photo viewer and drawing viewer (<code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">role="dialog"</span></code>, <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">aria-modal</span></code>) are the only modal surfaces of our own.</p></li> <li><p>No transition or entrance longer than 260 ms, no motion that carries meaning, none under reduced motion. The exceptions are the two looping indicators, which stop under reduced motion: the busy icon’s pulse (0.8 s) and the image placeholder’s shimmer (1.2 s).</p></li> <li><p>No touch target under 48 px where it can be avoided (the 36 px badge, the 30 px remove button and the 42 px <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.chips</span></code> segments extend their hit area with an invisible <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">::after</span></code>). The exceptions: the <code class="docutils literal notranslate"><span class="pre">.facet</span></code> chips and the tag-field inputs in the Filters panel and the tag editor are 44 px high; the embed chip’s ✕ is 40 px wide (48 high); and the Photo / Drawing tabs are 42 px segments in a 48 px track, with no extension. No text under 12.75 px, except the 0.7rem (about 12 px) count on the Filters button, which is not interactive and is also in the button’s name; no text pair under AA.</p></li> <li><p>No dimming to mean “busy”: the label (the text) is never dimmed, since opacity would break contrast; it says “Preparing…” in words, and only the control’s icon pulses.</p></li> </ul> </section> </section>
